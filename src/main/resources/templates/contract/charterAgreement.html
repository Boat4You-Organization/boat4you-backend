Terms & Conditions

Annex 1 to Charter Booking Agreement #000000/2026

These Terms govern the brokerage services of Cusmanich d.o.o., trading as Boat4You (the “Broker”), and record the framework of the charter booked with the Operator. By proceeding with payment, the Charterer accepts these Terms in full.

1.Nature of this Agreement and role of the parties

1.1 This Agreement records the booking of the Yacht described on page 1 and governs the brokerage services provided by the Broker. The Broker acts solely as a booking intermediary, in the name and on behalf of the Operator identified on page 1.

1.2 The contract for the charter of the Yacht (the “Charter Contract”) is concluded directly between the Charterer and the Operator. The Broker is not a party to the Charter Contract, does not own, operate, crew or maintain the Yacht, and does not perform the charter.

1.3 All obligations concerning the condition, seaworthiness, equipment, insurance, availability and performance of the Yacht, and the conduct of any crew provided, rest exclusively with the Operator.

2.Operator’s Charter Terms

2.1 The performance of the charter is governed by the Operator’s general terms and conditions of charter (the “Operator’s Charter Terms”), which are provided to the Charterer together with this Agreement or, at the latest, at check-in, where the Charterer confirms them in writing as part of the standard check-in procedure.

2.2 In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the Operator’s Charter Terms concerning the Yacht or the performance of the charter, the Operator’s Charter Terms prevail. This Agreement prevails only in respect of the brokerage services.

2.3 On request, the Broker will supply the Charterer with a copy of the Operator’s Charter Terms before payment of the deposit.

3.Charter fee and payment

3.1 The charter fee is set by the Operator and covers the use of the Yacht for the charter period stated on page 1, including the equipment listed in the Yacht’s standard inventory. Optional extras and base charges (transit log, end cleaning, skipper, fuel, water toys and similar) are payable separately, as shown on page 1 or in the Operator’s price list.

3.2 Payment is due in the instalments and by the deadlines stated on page 1. All payments made to the Broker are received by the Broker in the name and for the account of the Operator and are applied to the charter fee. Bank and card-processing charges are borne by the Charterer as shown on page 1.

3.3 The booking is final and binding once the deposit payment is received. If the final balance is not received by its due date, the booking may be cancelled and cancellation charges applied in accordance with Section 13.

3.4 Upon receipt of the full charter fee, the Broker issues a Charter Voucher confirming the booking, including the Operator’s base and 24-hour contact details. The Voucher is presented to the Operator at check-in.

4.Security deposit

4.1 A refundable security deposit in the amount stated on page 1 (or, failing that, in the Operator’s yacht specification) is payable directly to the Operator at the latest at check-in, in the form and currency requested by the Operator.

4.2 The deposit is held by the Operator and refunded at check-out in accordance with the Operator’s Charter Terms, provided the Yacht and its equipment are returned undamaged, complete and with the agreed fuel level.

4.3 The deposit generally corresponds to the insurance excess; the Charterer’s liability for damage and loss is governed by the Operator’s Charter Terms and the applicable insurance policy. The Broker is not a party to the deposit transaction and has no liability for amounts retained by the Operator.

5.Check-in, documents and check-out

5.1 Check-in and check-out take place at the times and location stated on page 1. At check-in the Charterer must present valid identity documents for all crew, a complete crew list as required by the local authorities, and — for bareboat charters — the skipper documents referred to in Section 6.

5.2 The Yacht is handed over after a joint inventory check between the Charterer and the Operator, recorded in the check-in protocol. The Charterer must verify the inventory and report any discrepancy or visible defect in the check-in protocol before leaving the marina.

5.3 Late return entitles the Operator to apply the late-return charges set out in the Operator’s Charter Terms, together with any documented consequential costs.

6.Skipper qualifications (bareboat charters)

6.1 For bareboat charters, the Charterer (or the nominated skipper named in the booking) must hold a sailing licence and, where required, a VHF radio operator certificate, valid and recognised by the competent maritime authority of the charter area. Recognition requirements differ by country; on request, the Broker will advise which certificates are accepted in the charter area, but responsibility for holding valid, recognised documents rests with the Charterer.

6.2 The Charterer warrants that the skipper has the practical experience required to handle the booked Yacht in local conditions. The Operator may refuse to release the Yacht if, at check-in, the required documents are missing or the skipper’s competence is reasonably judged inadequate; in that case a professional skipper may be required at the Charterer’s expense, in accordance with the Operator’s Charter Terms.

7.Skippered and crewed charters

7.1 Where a skipper, hostess or other crew is booked, they are provided and engaged by the Operator. Their fees are shown on page 1 or in the Operator’s price list; their provisioning is paid by the Charterer unless otherwise stated.

7.2 The skipper has final authority over navigation, routing and the safety of the Yacht and the persons on board. The Charterer will follow the skipper’s reasonable instructions at all times.

8.Use of the Yacht

8.1 The Yacht may be used solely for private leisure cruising. Commercial use, sub-charter, carriage of paying passengers, instruction and participation in races (unless expressly agreed with the Operator) are prohibited.

8.2 The number of persons on board, including children, must not exceed the Yacht’s certified capacity, and the crew list must be kept accurate as required by the local authorities.

8.3 The Charterer must navigate within the cruising area permitted by the competent local authority and the Yacht’s insurance policy. Leaving that area, night sailing where restricted, or crossing into foreign territorial waters requires the Operator’s prior written consent.

9.Insurance

9.1 The Yacht is insured by the Operator against hull damage and third-party liability as required by the law of the charter area. The scope of cover, exclusions and the excess are set out in the Operator’s Charter Terms and insurance documents.

9.2 Personal effects and valuables of the Charterer and crew are not covered by the Yacht’s insurance. The Broker strongly recommends personal travel and charter-cancellation insurance.

9.3 Damage caused by gross negligence, operation under the influence of alcohol or drugs, sailing despite official weather warnings, or navigation outside the permitted area is typically excluded from insurance cover and is payable by the Charterer under the Operator’s Charter Terms.

10.Defects, repairs and remedies during the charter

10.1 The Charterer must operate the Yacht with ordinary care and perform the routine daily checks described at check-in (engine oil, coolant, bilges, rigging and similar).

10.2 Any defect, damage or malfunction occurring during the charter must be reported to the Operator without undue delay, using the Operator’s base and 24-hour contact numbers stated on the Voucher and in the Yacht’s documents, with a copy to the Broker where practicable. The Operator will organise repair as soon as reasonably practicable. Repairs ordered by the Charterer without the Operator’s prior consent are not reimbursable.

10.3 Remedies for defects — including any pro-rata reduction or refund where a defect not caused by the Charterer materially impairs the use of the Yacht and cannot be remedied within a reasonable time (typically 24 hours) — are governed by the Operator’s Charter Terms and applicable law, and constitute claims against the Operator. At the Charterer’s request, the Broker will assist in submitting, documenting and following up such claims with the Operator.

10.4 Indirect and consequential losses (flights, accommodation, lost holiday time and similar) are excluded to the extent permitted by applicable law.

11.Weather and force majeure

11.1 The Charterer must observe official weather forecasts and the instructions of the local authorities. Time lost due to adverse weather, strikes, government action, epidemics, war or other events of force majeure does not entitle the Charterer to a refund or an extension of the charter, except as provided in the Operator’s Charter Terms or by mandatory law.

12.Local rules and conduct on board

12.1 The Charterer must comply with the laws and environmental rules of the charter area, including waste-discharge rules and the rules of any marine protected area visited. Penalties imposed by the authorities as a result of the Charterer’s conduct are payable by the Charterer.

12.2 Pets are allowed only with the Operator’s prior written consent and against the applicable fee. Smoking below deck and open flames on board are prohibited. Conduct that endangers the Yacht, its crew or third parties may lead to termination of the charter by the Operator in accordance with the Operator’s Charter Terms.

13.Cancellation by the Charterer

13.1 Cancellation must be communicated to the Broker in writing (e-mail is sufficient). The date of receipt determines the applicable cancellation charge.

13.2 Cancellation charges are those of the Operator, as stated on the booking offer or confirmation. Unless stated otherwise there, the following default schedule applies, calculated on the total charter fee:
(a)  more than 60 days before check-in — 30% of the charter fee;
(b)  60 to 30 days before check-in — 50% of the charter fee;
(c)  30 to 14 days before check-in — 80% of the charter fee;
(d)  fewer than 14 days before check-in, no-show or termination after check-in — 100% of the charter fee.

13.3 Cancellation charges are applied and retained in the name and for the account of the Operator. If the cancelled period is re-sold to another client at the same price and for the same period, the Charterer is refunded the difference between the amounts paid and the charges actually retained, less an administrative fee of 10% of the charter fee.

13.4 The Broker strongly recommends charter-cancellation insurance.

14.Non-availability of the Yacht

14.1 If the booked Yacht becomes unavailable for reasons not attributable to the Charterer, the Operator is obliged to provide a replacement yacht of equivalent or superior specification or, failing that, to refund all amounts paid for the charter. The Broker will use its best efforts to secure a suitable replacement — from the Operator or from another operator — and will promptly forward to the Charterer any refunds received on the Charterer’s behalf.

14.2 Where all amounts paid are refunded and no replacement is accepted, no further claim can be made against the Broker. Claims for additional loss, if any, are governed by the Operator’s Charter Terms and applicable law.

15.Complaints

15.1 Complaints concerning the condition of the Yacht, its equipment or the performance of the charter must be submitted in writing to the Operator — at check-in in the check-in protocol, during the charter via the Operator’s contact numbers, and at the latest in the check-out protocol — with a copy to the Broker. Timely written notification at the base is a condition of the Operator’s complaint procedure; late claims may not be entertained.

15.2 The Broker will assist the Charterer in submitting, documenting and following up any such complaint with the Operator and will coordinate communication between the parties.

15.3 Complaints concerning the Broker’s own services (booking, payment processing, information provided) may be submitted in writing to info@boat4you.com. The Broker responds in writing within 15 days of receipt.

16.Liability of the Broker

16.1 The Broker’s obligations are limited to: accurately transmitting the booking and the Charterer’s information to the Operator; forwarding payments received in the name and for the account of the Operator; issuing the Charter Voucher; and providing the assistance described in Sections 14 and 15.

16.2 The Broker is not liable for the condition, seaworthiness, equipment, availability or performance of the Yacht, for the acts or omissions of the Operator or its crew, or for the accuracy of information supplied by the Operator, except where the Broker has failed to transmit such information with due care.

16.3 To the extent permitted by law, the Broker’s aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the charter fee actually paid by the Charterer, and indirect or consequential losses are excluded. Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits liability that cannot be excluded or limited under applicable mandatory law.

17.Personal data

17.1 The Broker processes the personal data of the Charterer and crew for the purpose of the booking and shares it with the Operator and, where required, with port and maritime authorities. Details are set out in the Broker’s privacy policy at www.boat4you.com/privacy.

18.Governing law and disputes

18.1 This Agreement (the brokerage relationship) is governed by the law of the Republic of Croatia. The courts of Split, Croatia have jurisdiction, unless mandatory consumer-protection rules of the Charterer’s country of residence provide otherwise.

18.2 The Charter Contract between the Charterer and the Operator is governed by the law and jurisdiction stated in the Operator’s Charter Terms.

18.3 Consumers resident in the European Union may also submit complaints through the European Online Dispute Resolution platform at ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr.

19.1 This Agreement is concluded electronically and is valid and binding without a handwritten signature or stamp. Payment of the deposit constitutes acceptance of this Agreement in full.
